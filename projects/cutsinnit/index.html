CutsInnit — Web Design

Type Web Design
Year 2026
Date January 2026
Role Design & Build

A friend from my residence ran a barber business out of it, cutting clients from across Montreal. He asked me for a portfolio of his work — somewhere people could see the cuts he offers and the prices, understand exactly what he does, and find him online in the first place.

The site answers that in one run: the work first, then the services and their prices, then booking. Rather than send people to a phone number, I built booking into the page against a set schedule — press Book and his actual availability comes up, so a client picks a slot and is done.
