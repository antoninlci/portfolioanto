About

Katie

As a certified nail artist based in Montreal, I specialize in the art of the perfect set. My approach focuses on meticulous nail care, which is why every service includes a flawless Russian Manicure to ensure healthy natural nails and long-lasting gel extensions.

Whether you are looking for minimalist elegance or complex custom art — incorporating 3D designs, chrome, or charms — every detail is tailored to your vision. Welcome to a warm, private space where your ideas are expertly brought to life.

Cat-friendly household — Please take into consideration if you have allergies.
