Vantage is built for clothing brands. Every screen is made for the garment: a product page that shows it and nothing else, a collection grid that lets the photography carry the page, and a cart that opens over the page instead of pulling the customer away from what they were looking at.

It fits a ten-piece label as well as a full catalogue — collections, search, cart, customer accounts, and a page for everything a store has to say. Sections are added, reordered and removed in the editor, so the shop follows the brand and not the other way round.

It ships with its own font, five languages, and no dependency on a paid app. Everything visible is a setting in the Shopify editor.

Questions
